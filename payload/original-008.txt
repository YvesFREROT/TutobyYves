<section class="section">
  <h2 data-toc="1">6. Chat and GPT-5.6</h2>
  <p class="lead">Chat remains the immediate conversational space for questions, synthesis, research, brainstorming and rapid drafting. On desktop it now sits next to Work and Codex in the same application.</p>
  <p>GPT-5.6 Sol powers Medium and High reasoning, and Extra High where the plan allows it. The practical difference is the amount of deliberation used before answering.</p>
  <div class="table-wrap"><table><thead><tr><th>Level</th><th>Use it for</th><th>Trade-off</th></tr></thead><tbody>
    <tr><td>Instant / default</td><td>Simple questions, rewriting, quick summaries and first-pass exploration.</td><td>Fastest, but less suitable for multi-constraint decisions.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Medium</td><td>Comparisons, structured recommendations and moderate analysis.</td><td>More time and usage than Instant.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>High</td><td>Complex decisions, deep document analysis and demanding planning.</td><td>Slower; still requires human verification.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Extra High</td><td>Highest-effort reasoning on eligible plans.</td><td>Plan-dependent and the most resource intensive.</td></tr>
  </tbody></table></div>
  <div class="callout"><strong>Recommended method</strong>Use Instant for orientation, Medium or High for the decision, then ask for a contradictory review. A higher reasoning level does not remove the need to check sources, numbers and assumptions.</div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2 data-toc="1">7. Chat tutorial — produce a reliable recommendation</h2>
  <div class="steps">
    <div class="step">Open Chat on web, mobile or the unified desktop app.</div>
    <div class="step">Attach the approved source files and state which external sources are allowed.</div>
    <div class="step">Ask for a comparison grid before requesting the recommendation.</div>
    <div class="step">Require facts, assumptions and judgments to be separated.</div>
    <div class="step">Run a second pass that tries to invalidate the first answer.</div>
    <div class="step">Verify the critical figures and citations before reusing the output.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="prompt"><div class="prompt-title">Main prompt</div><pre>I am attaching three supplier proposals. Compare them on total cost, delivery time, contractual risk, reversibility, security requirements and hidden costs. Cite the source for each material statement. End with a recommendation, confidence level and the missing information that could change the decision. Separate facts, assumptions and judgments.</pre></div>
  <div class="prompt"><div class="prompt-title">Adversarial check</div><pre>Act as a critical reviewer. Identify the three weakest parts of the recommendation, build a plausible scenario in which another option becomes preferable, and list the human checks required before approval.</pre></div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2 data-toc="1">8. Plugin Directory and reusable workflows</h2>
  <p>The Plugin Directory replaces the App Directory as the discovery point for reusable workflows in ChatGPT and Codex. A plugin can combine Skills, connected apps and app templates. Existing app connections are not automatically removed.</p>
  <div class="steps">
    <div class="step">Open Plugins from the sidebar or Settings &gt; Plugins.</div>
    <div class="step">Read the plugin’s capabilities, data access, required app connections and administrative restrictions.</div>
    <div class="step">Connect only the underlying services that are necessary.</div>
    <div class="step">Invoke the plugin with an @ mention or the + menu, depending on the surface.</div>
    <div class="step">Require explicit approval before sending, publishing, deleting or modifying external data.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="prompt"><div class="prompt-title">Example with a messaging plugin</div><pre>@Slack, find project-channel messages since Monday. Summarise only decisions, blockers, owners and dates. Cite the source messages. Do not post or modify anything in Slack without my explicit approval.</pre></div>
  <div class="callout"><strong>Skill versus plugin</strong>A Skill contains instructions, examples and sometimes code for a consistent workflow. A plugin is a broader package that may include several Skills, apps and templates.</div>
</section>
