  <h2 data-toc="1">12. Work tutorial — monthly reporting workflow</h2>
  <p class="lead">Use case: consolidate monthly sales, pipeline, customer feedback and incidents, then produce a checked workbook, management memo and presentation.</p>
  <div class="steps">
    <div class="step"><strong>Prepare context.</strong> Create a dedicated project, add approved templates and connect only required sources.</div>
    <div class="step"><strong>Define acceptance criteria.</strong> Specify audience, deadline, KPI definitions, tolerances and mandatory checks.</div>
    <div class="step"><strong>Request a plan first.</strong> Ask Work to show its source list, proposed method and blocking questions before creating files.</div>
    <div class="step"><strong>Reconcile data.</strong> Require duplicate detection, unexplained variances, missing periods and a reconciliation table.</div>
    <div class="step"><strong>Generate outputs.</strong> Produce the workbook, memo and slides using the validated figures.</div>
    <div class="step"><strong>Run three reviews.</strong> Check numerical consistency, source traceability and executive readability.</div>
    <div class="step"><strong>Schedule the next cycle.</strong> Create a monthly task that alerts only when every required source is available.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="prompt"><div class="prompt-title">Master Work prompt</div><pre>You are responsible for monthly reporting. Before acting, present the plan, source list and required approvals. Then:
1. reconcile figures from authorised files and apps;
2. flag every inconsistency or missing period;
3. create a traceable workbook;
4. write a six-page executive memo;
5. create a ten-slide presentation;
6. perform a final cross-check.
Do not modify, publish or send any source without my explicit approval.</pre></div>
